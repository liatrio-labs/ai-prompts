Horizontal · reverse-color · preferred for dark surfaces PREFERRED
Use on the default dark canvas (`#1A1F23`). Flame stays full-color, type goes white. Clear-space ≥ height of the "L".
Horizontal · full-color · preferred for light surfaces PREFERRED
Use on white or light editorial surfaces (pricing, careers, case-study covers). Type sits in grey, flame in green.
Stacked · reverse-color · secondary SECONDARY
For square or near-square placements only — avatars, app icons, footer corners. Don't use as the primary header lockup.
Stacked · full-color · secondary SECONDARY
Light-surface counterpart to the stacked reverse. Same usage rules — never as the primary lockup.
